---

We are excited to share that our paper, "Air-permeable hydrogels through viscoelastic phase separation of aerogels," has been published in *Nature*! The work introduces a viscoelastic phase separation strategy to create highly air-permeable, water-rich hydrogels.

Congratulations to our PhD student Haodong Hu and undergraduate summer scholar Gabriella Carreira for their contributions as co-authors!

[Read the paper in Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10712-3)
