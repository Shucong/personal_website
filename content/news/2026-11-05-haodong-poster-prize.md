Congratulations to **Haodong Hu** on winning third place and a **$350 prize** at the School of Materials Science and Engineering at Georgia Tech Fall 2026 Poster Showcase. With only four prizes awarded among 50 entries, this recognition celebrates Haodong’s research and presentation.

We are proud of Haodong’s accomplishment and continued progress in materials science research.
