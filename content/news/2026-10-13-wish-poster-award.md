---

Congratulations to high school student mentee **Allen Seok** on receiving a Poster Award from Georgia Tech's Wearable Intelligent Systems and Healthcare (WISH) Center. Mentored by **Shiqi Wei** and **Siddharth K Sivakumar**, Allen presented their work on air-permeable hydrogel electrodes for wearable physiological monitoring. The project aims to make wearable sensors more breathable and comfortable without sacrificing signal quality.

We are grateful to Shiqi and Siddharth for their mentorship and to the WISH Center organizers for a wonderful event.
