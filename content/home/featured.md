  <h3>
    Air-permeable hydrogels through viscoelastic phase separation of aerogels
  </h3>

  <p>
    We use viscoelastic phase separation to create interconnected aerogel pathways within water-rich hydrogels, enabling efficient gas transport while preserving the soft and hydrated characteristics of the material.
  </p>

  <p>
    This work establishes internal phase architecture as a powerful design parameter for controlling transport in hydrogels and opens new opportunities for breathable soft interfaces and functional hydrogel devices.
  </p>
